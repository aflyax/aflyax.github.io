150 custom matte charcoal Go stones for OGS, 512x512 RGBA.

These are NOT a new generated stone texture set. They use exactly the previous
150 individual stones from OGS_150_Black_Matte_Clean_No_Halos_v3.zip, with
only an outer 32-pixel radial edge-band correction and a clean anti-aliased
circular alpha mask. Texture, central shading, and stone identity are
preserved. Black RGB halos have been avoided at the transparent edge.

Files black_001.png through black_150.png.
The final ZIP has NOT been uploaded to GitHub.
