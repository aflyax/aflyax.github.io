OGS AGED STRIPED CLAMSHELL WHITE STONES - V4 - 768PX

540 individual transparent 768x768 RGBA PNG files, white_001.png to white_540.png.
Designed for OGS custom White Stone variants. All files show the striped shell side.
Based on the original 540 V3 textures; existing band designs and orientations preserved.
Changes: 2x upscaled with Lanczos filtering (384 to 768), a modest warm-ivory and honey
color increase, slightly clearer native band contrast, consistent matte diffuse upper-left
lighting and gently offset transparent contact shade. Clean antialiased circular edges.
No chipped edges, extra artificial stripe designs, glossy shine, or full dark borders.

Technical honesty: upscaling increases pixel dimensions, but does not restore
fine material details absent in the 384px original textures.
Do not upload this ZIP itself to OGS: unpack PNGs and upload them to a public image host
(such as an appropriately named folder on GitHub Pages), then paste their raw URLs into OGS.
